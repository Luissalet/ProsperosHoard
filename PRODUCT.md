# Prospero's Hoard

## Platform

web

## Users

Luis uses this local creative studio to generate and assemble images, videos,
music and voice. The October 5 brief delegates the redesign and model choices
for an unattended improvement pass. A successful interface supports ordinary
visual inspection, including opening a small picture at full size with one click.

## Product Purpose

Produce editable, resumable audiovisual work with a local media library,
consistent cast, shot planning, generation, assembly and export. Help the user
understand what is ready, what failed and what action comes next.

## Operating Context

Existing Windows installation: Python/FastAPI backend, React/TypeScript/Vite
frontend, ComfyUI generation, ffmpeg assembly and local job storage. The same
operations are exposed through the agent API and MCP. Existing project files
and their references are evidence and must be preserved.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve all existing routes and operations: projects, cast and character kits,
image/video generation, library and viewer, design, audio, timeline, boards,
node spaces and apps, cinema guide, music-video and short productions, recipes,
voice, jobs, backends, activity, settings, imports, exports and recoverable trash.
Preserve Spanish and English, and dark and light modes.

No documentation is compulsory. Reference roles, imported scripts and lyrics,
segmentation and text/time links are optional aids. Prioritize immediate
creation from an idea or a few references. Preserve and improve canonical
identity and background cast continuity, including user-selected motion guides.

The user reports freezing between clips, weak motion and visual inconsistency.
The pass must inspect real video output and exercise the interface as a user.
Synthetic demo generation is suitable for interaction checks, never evidence
of model quality. Downloading models is authorized; paid external services,
publication and removal of original work are outside this improvement pass.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the product name and the original dragon-and-wand icon unchanged. The user
explicitly pins its magenta/pink brand palette and highlights: use those colors
for actions, focus and selection, never orange/copper. Preserve the inherited
Prospero palette in dark and light modes. Magnific informs workflows, not brand
colors or a replacement icon.

## Evidence on Hand

Repository, existing productions and local model installation; baseline user
journey and media measurements under D:/LocalAI/qa/prospero-redesign-20261005.

## Product Principles

- The work occupies the screen; infrastructure detail appears when relevant.
- Inspect any visual reference or output in one click without losing the task.
- Make generation stages, selected takes and next steps explicit.
- Preserve originals and expose the reason behind quality warnings.
- Report measured defects separately from subjective model limitations.
