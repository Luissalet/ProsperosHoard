# Production handoffs

Prospero declares handoffs from scene brief to concept image, from concept to character sheet, and from sheet to video. The Hub can include these in a chain and list or switch the generation and editing modules by their native controls. Compare the exported views and play the clip before handing it on. The video handoff uses the scene dossier and character sheet to plan anchored shots. Its video-clip provenance names the dossier, sheet, ordered takes and their fingerprints. The cut remains provisional until all its mounted takes pass continuity review.
